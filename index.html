Good to know

  • The subsidy is paid straight to your bank account (DBT).
  • Panels must be made in India (ALMM list) and fitted by a registered vendor.
  • Collateral-free loans at about 7% interest are available for systems up to 3 kW.
  • With net metering, extra units go to the grid.

What you need to apply

  • Registered mobile number
  • Electricity consumer account number
  • Bank account
  • Email and address

    Government schemes

    Two schemes cover homes and farms.

    PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

    Subsidy for rooftop solar on houses.

    • ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW
    • ₹18,000 per kW for additional capacity up to 3 kW
    • Capped at ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above

    PM-KUSUM

    For farmers to install solar pumps and grid-connected solar on farmland. The cost is shared by the Centre, the State and the farmer.

    • Promote awareness of solar energy.
    • Encourage rooftop panels, solar water heaters, solar pumps and solar street lights.

    • Explain the government schemes.

    Solar basics & myths

    Questions we heard most, answered.

    How does a solar panel work?

    Photovoltaic (PV) cells turn sunlight into DC power. An inverter converts it to AC for your home.

    On-grid, off-grid or hybrid?

    On-grid connects to the power line and has no battery. Off-grid runs on a battery. Hybrid has both.

    What is net metering?

    Extra power your panels make goes to the grid, and your bill reduces.

    How long do panels last?

    About 25 years. Just keep them clean.

    Myth: solar does not work on cloudy days

    It does work, with lower output.

    Myth: solar is only for the rich

    The subsidy covers a big part of the cost for small systems.
